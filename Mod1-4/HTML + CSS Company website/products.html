Our fresh menu

Everything on our menu is made fresh to order, from crispy chicken to warm, gooey cinnamon rolls. Pick your favorite or try them all.

Sizzling Cheeseburgers

Juicy beef patties smashed on the grill until crisp at the edges, then topped with melted cheese on a soft toasted bun. Hot, savory and satisfying.

Hot Honey Chicken Burger

Crispy fried chicken glazed with spicy hot honey, topped with crunchy lettuce and tucked into a toasted bun. Sweet heat in every bite.

Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, warm cinnamon rolls baked fresh and finished with a thick swirl of creamy cream cheese frosting. The sweetest way to end your meal.
